An optical heart-rate monitor built around a PIC32, with an analog front end and a Python display.

From light to pulse

I used an IR LED and phototransistor to pick up changes associated with a heartbeat. Cascaded LM358 op-amp stages amplify and condition the signal, and a Schmitt trigger produces a digital pulse for the microcontroller.

Firmware & display

The PIC32 sends measurements over UART to a Python interface that plots the pulse and BPM. A heart icon, LED, and buzzer provide heartbeat-synchronized feedback.
