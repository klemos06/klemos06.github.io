Chem E Car electronics

I contributed to the circuitry team for UBC’s Chem E Car, working on the PCB, assembly, and sensor integration.

My workPCB design support, assembly & sensor integration
Tools & hardwareAltium Designer · Arduino Nano · SMT & THT

PCB & assembly

I used Altium Designer to contribute to the car’s custom PCB and component selection around its power needs and the requirements of other subteams. I assembled surface-mount and through-hole components and used continuity checks to troubleshoot connections.

Reaction sensing

I integrated an Arduino Nano and calibrated the sensing code to detect small changes in the chemical reaction. The sensor output was used for responsive control of the car’s propulsion.
