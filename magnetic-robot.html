Robot & remote

I worked on the STM32-based IR receiver and decoder, measuring pulse widths to reconstruct custom packets from the remote. The handheld controller provides manual inputs, haptic feedback, and its own power supply.

Motor-drive circuitry

The drive electronics use H-bridge circuits with MOSFETs and optocouplers. The isolation separates the microcontroller from the motor-drive circuitry and its electrical noise.
