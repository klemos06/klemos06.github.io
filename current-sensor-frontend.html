Analog hardware

Current-sensor analog front end

A small analog board for conditioning current-sensor measurements before they reach the acquisition hardware.

My workSchematic design & 4-layer PCB routing
Tools & hardwareKiCad · INA821 · MCP6002 · OPA2320

Two paths from the same sensor

One path uses an INA821 instrumentation amplifier and a 50 Hz, second-order Sallen-Key filter. The other uses OPA2320 buffers to feed an MCC172 acquisition module. I included a buffered reference and test points for checking the signals during bring-up.
